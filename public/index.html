How these numbers are calculatedPricing, attribution & data coverage

Usage is measured. Dollars are estimated.

Costs use a Standard API rate card. Your Codex invoice can differ. Cache writes use the 30-minute rate where retention is unknown. Subscription fees, tool fees, and special processing or regional adjustments are not included.

Projects are named root conversations. Only their own usage and new work by linked helpers or forks are added. Separate conversations are not merged just because they have similar titles.
